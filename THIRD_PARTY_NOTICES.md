## Libraries

All library dependencies are under permissive licences compatible with AGPL-3.0. CI runs a licence check that
fails on any AGPL, GPL, SSPL, BUSL or source-available library dependency.

- **Web (Node)**: mostly MIT, Apache-2.0, ISC and BSD. Notable: React, Next.js (MIT), Ant Design and
  @ant-design/icons (MIT), Apache ECharts (Apache-2.0), next-intl (MIT), MapLibre GL JS (BSD-3-Clause),
  pmtiles and @protomaps/basemaps (BSD-3-Clause), adhan (MIT). Development only: axe-core and lightningcss
  (MPL-2.0), caniuse-lite (CC-BY-4.0).
- **Go**: pgx (MIT), adhan-go (MIT), go-hijri (MIT), minio-go (Apache-2.0, used as a generic S3 client),
  go-jose (Apache-2.0), golang.org/x (BSD-3-Clause), google/uuid (BSD-3-Clause), klauspost/compress
  (BSD-3-Clause and Apache-2.0), yaml.v3 (MIT and Apache-2.0).
- **Python engines**: numpy and scipy (BSD-3-Clause), LightGBM (MIT), OR-Tools (Apache-2.0), protobuf
  (BSD-3-Clause). The optional research image adds statsmodels (BSD-3-Clause), Prophet (MIT) and PyTorch
  (BSD-3-Clause; its wheel bundles libgomp under the GPL with the GCC runtime library exception).

To list every package with its licence: `pnpm licenses list -r`, `go-licenses report ./...` in `apps/api-go`,
and `pip-licenses` in the engines environment.
