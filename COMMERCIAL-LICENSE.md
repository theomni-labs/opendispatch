# Commercial licence

OpenDispatch is dual licensed.

## The open-source licence: AGPL-3.0

The whole project is available under the [GNU Affero General Public License, version 3](LICENSE)
(`AGPL-3.0-only`). You may use, study, change and share it, including commercially. The AGPL has one condition
that matters most for a server product: if you run a modified version and let people use it over a network,
you must offer those users the complete source code of your modified version, under the same licence.

For most evaluations, internal deployments of an unmodified release, research and contributions, the AGPL is
all you need.

## The commercial licence

Omni Labs also offers OpenDispatch under a commercial licence, for organisations that cannot or do not want to
meet the AGPL's conditions. Typical reasons:

- You want to modify OpenDispatch and run it as a service without publishing your changes.
- You want to embed OpenDispatch, or parts of it such as the engines, in a product you distribute under other
  terms.
- Your organisation's policy does not allow AGPL software.

A commercial licence can also include support, maintenance releases and help with deployment. Terms are agreed
case by case.

## How dual licensing works

Omni Labs holds the rights needed to offer both licences. Outside contributions are accepted under the
[contributor licence agreement](CLA.md), which lets Omni Labs distribute each contribution under the AGPL and
under commercial terms. Contributors keep the copyright in their work.

Choosing the commercial licence does not change the open-source licence for anyone else. Every release stays
available under the AGPL.
